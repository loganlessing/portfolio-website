About

I started college studying electrical engineering at Michigan State, but Formula SAE shifted my perspective. Being part of the design and manufacturing process, and seeing what it actually takes to go from a concept to a finished part, is what drew me toward Industrial and Systems Engineering at Oakland University, where I'm concentrating in engineering management and production systems.

On the technical side I work primarily in NX CAD/CAM, Fusion 360, and ANSYS for structural analysis, with some programming in C++ and JavaScript in the mix. I also spend a good amount of time working with AI tools, finding ways to fold them into real engineering and project workflows rather than just using them as a shortcut.
