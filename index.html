About
Logan Lessing
Oakland University

I started college studying electrical engineering at Michigan State, but Formula SAE shifted my perspective. Being part of the design and manufacturing process — seeing what it actually takes to go from a concept to a finished part — is what drew me toward Industrial and Systems Engineering at Oakland University, where I'm concentrating in engineering management and production systems.

On the technical side I work primarily in NX CAD/CAM, Fusion 360, and ANSYS for structural analysis, with some programming in C++ and JavaScript in the mix. I also spend a good amount of time finding ways to fold AI tools into real engineering and project workflows.
